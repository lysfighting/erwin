# Harness Proposal Review Template

> Status: Active
>
> Date: 2026-04-10
>
> Purpose: provide a canonical template for deeper file-backed proposal reviews when a ledger note is no longer enough.

---

## Header

- `Proposal`
- `Date`
- `Purpose`

---

## Review Scope

Name:

- the proposal being reviewed
- the main weekly / knowledge / feedback anchors being used
- the narrower question this review should answer

---

## Active Thread Anchor

Include:

- `Primary Thread`
- optional `Supporting Thread`
- why these threads are the right durable anchors
- the specific upstream anchors used in the review
- a one-line result

---

## Benchmark Evidence Check

Use when benchmark-heavy evidence materially shapes the proposal.

Include:

- `Benchmark / Source`
- `Capability Signal`
- `Eval-Trust Caveat`
- `Safe Local Principle`
- `Proposal Posture Effect`
- `Cargo-Cult Guard`
- a one-line result

If benchmark-heavy evidence is not central, say that explicitly and skip the rest.

---

## Monitorability Criterion

Use when trajectory diagnosis, monitorability, or output-only-review limits materially shape the proposal.

Include:

- `Visible Stages`
- `Hidden States`
- `Safe Local Principle`
- a one-line result

If monitorability is not central, say that explicitly and skip the rest.

---

## Maturity Overclaim Check

Use when the proposal could be mistaken for proof of runtime or governance maturity.

Include:

- what stronger claim the repo should avoid making
- why the current evidence is or is not strong enough
- a one-line result

---

## Disposition Review

State:

- current disposition
- whether it should remain, sharpen, or change
- why

---

## Recommended Next Moves

List the smallest justified next moves.

Prefer:

- review or eval surfaces before runtime expansion
- bounded follow-through instead of general ambition

---

## Review Outcome

Close with:

- whether the proposal was strengthened, narrowed, or left unchanged
- what the repo now knows that it did not know before this review
