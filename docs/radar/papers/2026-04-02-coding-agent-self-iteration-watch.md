## Watch Set

### Self-correction and self-improvement loops

- `Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback`
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651
  - Why it matters: gives a canonical bounded self-feedback loop instead of unconstrained self-rewrite.

- `Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning`
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366
  - Why it matters: highlights feedback-memory-reflection patterns that are relevant to eval and council design.

- `Recursive Introspection: Teaching LLM Agents How to Self-Improve`
  - https://openreview.net/forum?id=ze0nodITam
  - Why it matters: directly tracks whether agents improve by learning from failed attempts.

### Benchmarks that matter to coding agents

- `MLE-bench: Evaluating Machine Learning Agents on Machine Learning Engineering`
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07095
  - Why it matters: makes long-horizon ML engineering a concrete benchmark surface instead of a vague aspiration.

- `PaperBench: Evaluating AI's Ability to Replicate AI Research`
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01848
  - Why it matters: connects coding agents, research replication, and self-improvement evaluation in one benchmark family.
