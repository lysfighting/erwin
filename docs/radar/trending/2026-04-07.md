## Why They May Be Surfacing

- The board is crowded with projects that make local or self-directed AI use more legible: on-device demos, local search, local inference, and open agent runtimes all showed up together.
- Several entries sit directly in the coding-agent and developer-tooling lane, including GitNexus, Goose, Obsidian Skills, and qmd, which suggests continued appetite for practical agent interfaces rather than model-only repos.
- Security and infrastructure angles were also visible through Shannon and the return of established inference stacks like Ollama and `llama.cpp`, so the board was broader than just assistant UX.
- `openscreen` and `immich` likely benefited from being immediately understandable, polished utilities even in a board otherwise dominated by AI projects.
