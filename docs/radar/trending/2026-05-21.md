## Why They May Be Surfacing

- Capability packaging is still a major demand center. `academic-research-skills`, `andrej-karpathy-skills`, `superpowers`, `agency-agents`, and `claude-plugins-official` all assume teams want installable operating bundles, not isolated prompt snippets.
- Context efficiency is becoming its own layer. `codegraph` and `agentmemory` both externalize reliability and cost control instead of hiding them inside the core agent loop.
- Terminal-native agent infrastructure remains hot. `CLI-Anything` and `oh-my-pi` both push the idea that existing software and local dev environments should become directly agent-usable.
- The board is still genuinely general-interest. `OpenWA`, `OpenToonz`, and `files.md` show that agent tooling is winning attention on merit rather than because the board was narrowed to an AI slice.
