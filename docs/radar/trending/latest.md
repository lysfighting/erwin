## Trending Snapshot

The default global board captured on 2026-07-18 is mixed but distinctly agent-aware. The front page is not AI-only: it still includes broad systems, infra, and tutorial projects. ## Why They May Be Surfacing

- Coding-agent infrastructure is hot again. [MoonshotAI/kimi-cli](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/kimi-cli), [KnockOutEZ/wigolo](https://github.com/KnockOutEZ/wigolo), and [tirth8205/code-review-graph](https://github.com/tirth8205/code-review-graph) all point at the same pressure: developers want better shells, research tools, and review surfaces around agentic coding.
- Operator visibility still matters. [PostHog/posthog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog) surfacing alongside agent projects reinforces that observability and experiment loops remain part of the stack, not cleanup work after launch.
- Output quality is being productized. [ibelick/ui-skills](https://github.com/ibelick/ui-skills) suggests design and presentation constraints are now portable artifacts that can sit next to agent workflows.
- The board is still globally representative. [Robbyant/lingbot-map](https://github.com/Robbyant/lingbot-map) and [codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) help show that today's board is not just an AI niche feed.
