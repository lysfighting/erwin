## Trending Snapshot

Today’s unfiltered board is still mixed, but the center of gravity is unusually close to agent operators and AI workflow packaging. The top set combines consumer AI-video tooling, coding-agent harnesses, reusable skills, anti-slop quality layers, document-ingestion utilities, and a few broader business or developer projects.

- [microsoft/markitdown](https://github.com/microsoft/markitdown): document-to-Markdown conversion utility; relevant because ingestion and normalization remain a practical agent substrate.
- [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers): durable execution and planning overlay for agents; still one of the clearest reusable operator-methodology repositories on the board.
- [revfactory/harness](https://github.com/revfactory/harness): smaller but directly relevant because it packages domain-specific agent teams and the skills they use.

## Why They May Be Surfacing

- Reusable operator layers are still sticky. `ECC`, `taste-skill`, `superpowers`, and `harness` all show that people are not only chasing models, but also packaging how those models should work.
- Quality pressure is becoming a product surface. `taste-skill` and `stop-slop` both exist to constrain weak or generic outputs, which is a clear downstream signal of broader frustration with default model behavior.
- Utility pipelines still matter. `markitdown` surfacing again reinforces that ingestion, normalization, and conversion layers remain high-leverage infrastructure for agent systems.
- The board is not pure AI monoculture. Projects like `twenty` still show that the default page rewards broader product and developer pull, even when AI/agent repos dominate the conversation.
