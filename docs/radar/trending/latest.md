# GitHub Trending - 2026-07-17

Capture source: [GitHub Trending](https://github.com/trending)

## Trending Snapshot

The default global board captured on 2026-07-17 is mixed rather than single-theme. AI and coding-agent projects are clearly present, but they sit beside broad developer tutorials, product infrastructure, media apps, and foundational libraries. The strongest visible clusters are coding-agent tooling, developer workflow surfaces, and “make software look or behave better” utility projects.

- [protocolbuffers/protobuf](https://github.com/protocolbuffers/protobuf)

## Why They May Be Surfacing

- Coding-agent infrastructure is still hot. `copilot-sdk`, `cwc-workshops`, `openinterpreter`, and `hallmark` all reflect growing demand for agent shells, agent extension points, and reusable operating patterns.
- Developer control surfaces remain valuable. `PostHog` is surfacing because observability, experiments, and operator feedback loops matter more as agents become less deterministic.
- The board is not just frontier-model fandom. `build-your-own-x` and `protobuf` show broad engineering interest still pushing classic learning and systems projects high on the board.
- The “quality of output” layer is becoming productized. `hallmark` surfacing alongside agent frameworks suggests that style, UX discipline, and reviewability are now part of the coding-agent ecosystem, not an afterthought.
