## Why They May Be Surfacing

- Capability packaging remains a major demand center. `claude-plugins-official`, `andrej-karpathy-skills`, `dotnet/skills`, and `superpowers` all assume teams want installable operating bundles rather than isolated prompts.
- Context efficiency is still becoming its own layer. `codegraph` shows that teams increasingly want memory and context compression to live outside the core agent loop.
- Terminal and browser control remain part of the same stack. `CLI-Anything` and `chrome-devtools-mcp` suggest operators still want agents to act directly on existing software surfaces rather than wait for every app to build a bespoke agent API.
- The board is still genuinely mixed. AI-agent infrastructure is strong today, but it is winning attention on a general-interest page rather than on a narrowed AI-only slice.
