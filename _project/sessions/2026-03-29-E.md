## Summary

Closed a major chunk of the remaining control-plane gap by moving four previously partial areas into the actual runtime:

This does not finish the entire long-term control-plane vision, but it moves the harness from “research says these should exist” to “runtime now materially enforces them.”

## Major outcomes

### 1. Ownership and overlap are now first-class runtime concerns

- runs and contracts now carry `ownership_claims`
- active exclusive overlap is rejected during teammate assignment unless explicitly shared
- `home` and `doctor` now surface overlap state instead of leaving it implicit

### 2. Desired state and dependency waiting are now stronger

- runs and contracts now carry `desired_state`
- `drive wait` now records dependency waiting directly
- `home` and `doctor` now surface dependency-blocked runs

### 3. Authority enforcement is stronger

- invalid authority levels are now rejected or flagged for signals and meeting findings
- authority is still not fully conflict-resolving across the whole harness, but it is no longer just advisory metadata

### 4. Councils can now advance phases

- meetings now support `orchestrate`
- councils can progress from setup → independent review → cross-examination → synthesis → output based on recorded packet/position/output state
