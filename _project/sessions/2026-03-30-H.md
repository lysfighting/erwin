# Session Log — 2026-03-30 H

## Summary

- Added stale explicit-governor detection to scheduler maintenance and doctor.
- Proved that an adopted bundle governor is no longer silently trusted after its governing mission falls out of the active mission graph.
- Kept the runtime clean by showing the stale signal can be cleared back into a fresh escalation-required surviving bundle.

## Why

Explicit bundle-governor adoption closed one side of the leader control loop, but a release-grade control plane also needs to recognize when that adopted governor has become stale. Without this, a durable `bundle_governor_established` signal could outlive the mission graph it was supposed to govern and keep misleading scheduler or authority surfaces.

## What Changed

- `internal/harness/runtime.go`
  - added invalid-explicit-governor detection based on the active mission graph
  - scheduler maintenance now emits `clear_invalid_bundle_governor` when an active explicit-governor signal no longer points at a live bundle or governing mission
  - `doctor` now reports `invalid-explicit-bundle-governor` when stale explicit governance lingers in runtime state
- `scripts/harness-smoke-test.sh`
  - extended explicit-governor coverage to close the adopted governor mission
  - verified scheduler surfaces stale explicit governance as maintenance
