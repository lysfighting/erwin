# Session Log — 2026-03-30 F

## Summary

- Made council orchestration genuinely protocol-aware instead of treating `decision_protocol` as metadata only.
- Taught `incident-command` and `decision-record` to defer same-authority disagreement into bounded synthesis/record output instead of auto-generating live route/round work.
- Preserved the richer `evidence-review` controller so the strongest debate automation still exists where it actually belongs.

## Why

`Harness` already had explicit decision protocols, but the runtime still pushed nearly every materially contested lane through the same debate engine. That kept `arch-review`-style evidence review too central even when the right method was incident stabilization or bounded decision recording.

The next step toward the final control-plane shape was to let protocol choice affect live orchestration semantics, not only role defaults and scheduler recommendations.

## What Changed

- `internal/harness/runtime.go`
  - added protocol-aware council helpers for direct-synthesis requirements, protocol-specific coverage notes, and protocol-specific follow-up action summaries
  - `meetingDebatePlan` now treats `evidence-review` as the fully debate-capable protocol
  - `incident-command` and `decision-record` no longer auto-generate cross-examination route obligations for same-authority conflict with no authority winner
  - when those protocols do see a bounded reply but still lack a winner, they stop further round generation and defer the lane to synthesis/record output instead of growing the debate loop
  - `meetingAutoSynthesize` now turns those protocol-deferred lanes into unresolved findings plus protocol-specific follow-up actions
  - `meetings orchestrate` now emits protocol-aware synthesis requirements rather than generic debate wording when the active protocol intentionally bypasses live route/round work
  - `meetings summary` now surfaces `protocol_deferred_route_count` and `protocol_bound_route_count`
- `harness/templates/meeting.yml`
  - extended debate-plan shape with protocol-deferred / protocol-bounded counters
- `scripts/harness-teammate-smoke-test.sh`
  - added protocol-aware coverage proving `incident-command` and `decision-record` can both advance from independent review directly to output for same-authority disagreement
  - verified those meetings produce bounded follow-up action output instead of cross-examination route obligations
