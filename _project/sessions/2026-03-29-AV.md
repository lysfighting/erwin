## Why It Matters

Before this session, debate batches were visible but not yet first-class execution state. The leader could see grouped obligations, but the runtime did not explicitly mark one batch as active, keep the others blocked, or prevent later phases from bypassing that active work. This session made debate batching behave more like a real controller.
