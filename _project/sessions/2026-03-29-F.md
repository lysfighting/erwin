## Summary

Closed the biggest remaining control-plane gap by moving mission state from “implied by runs” to a durable first-class object.

- introduced a mission registry / mission graph surface above runs

That means the harness no longer regulates only against runs.
It now has mission objects the leader can inspect directly, and existing repos do not need manual cleanup to benefit.
