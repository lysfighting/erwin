## Key Decisions

- **git add -u over -A**: Safer default — never stage untracked files automatically
- **sed portability via function**: `_sed_i` detects GNU/BSD at runtime, cleaner than temp-file approach
- **Stash pop by index**: Use `grep -oE 'stash@\{[0-9]+\}'` to find exact stash, not blind `pop`
