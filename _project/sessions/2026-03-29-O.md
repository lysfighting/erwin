# Session Log — 2026-03-29 O

## Summary

Deepened council automation by teaching `meetings orchestrate` to auto-synthesize provisional findings and unresolved follow-up actions from grouped council positions.

## What Changed

- `meetings add-position` now accepts:
  - `--claim=...`
  - `--dimension=...`
- `meetings orchestrate` now:
  - removes previously auto-generated synthesis artifacts before recomputing
  - preserves manual findings already on the record
  - groups positions by governed claim
  - auto-generates provisional `accepted` / `rejected` / `unresolved` findings from grouped positions
  - auto-generates unresolved follow-up actions when same-authority support/challenge positions still disagree
- `meetings summary` now surfaces:
  - `auto_generated_finding_count`
  - `auto_generated_action_count`
  - `auto_generated_claim_count`
- Teammate smoke now proves a council path where:
  - positions are grouped by claim
  - orchestration auto-generates findings
  - unresolved claims produce action items
  - the meeting can then advance cleanly to `output`

## Why It Matters

This closes a meaningful part of the gap between:

- “the council stored bounded debate inputs”
- and
- “the council can now synthesize provisional judgment from those inputs”

The system is still not a full automated debate controller, but it is now materially closer to the original `arch-review`-inspired design.
