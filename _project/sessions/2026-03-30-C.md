# Session Log — 2026-03-30 C

## Summary

- Reframed Harness decision governance so councils are no longer implicitly treated as one inherited `arch-review` ritual.
- Added explicit decision protocols to meetings and scheduler human-decision packets.
- Wrote a methodology synthesis doc that places `arch-review` alongside cybernetics, Kubernetes, incident command, and decision records instead of above them.

## Why

The architecture had already absorbed a lot from `arch-review`, but the control plane was at risk of drifting into “every important decision is an arch-review-style debate.” That was too narrow for the actual decision surfaces inside `Harness`, especially release-adjacent and lower-conflict design decisions.

## Outcome

`Harness` now makes its decision methodology explicit:

- `evidence-review` for contested technical judgment
- `incident-command` for urgent release/operational decisions
- `decision-record` for bounded design choices

That keeps the system aligned with the earlier research goal: absorb `arch-review` without letting it become the only semantics of leader/council governance.
